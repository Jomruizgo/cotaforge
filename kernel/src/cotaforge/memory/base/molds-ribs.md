---
id: molds-ribs
type: practice
title: Thin, low ribs, with a base radius and draft
sector: injection-molds
applies_when: When reinforcing a wall of an injection-molded part
not_when: A thick part that does not use ribs
recognize: A rib almost as thick as the wall, or taller than 3 times its thickness
typical_error: A sink mark on the face opposite the rib
related:
- design-short-direct-load-path
sources:
- "Hubs, Injection molding design guide (read)."
---
- Rib thickness: 0.5 times the thickness of the main wall.
- Height: less than 3 times the rib thickness. The same guide gives elsewhere "3 times the wall thickness" as the maximum height, which does not match: confirm with the molder which to use.
- Base radius greater than 1/4 of the rib thickness; draft of at least 0.25° to 0.5°; distance between ribs and walls of at least 4 times the rib thickness.
