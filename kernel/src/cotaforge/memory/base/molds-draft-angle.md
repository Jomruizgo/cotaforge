title: Draft angle on all faces parallel to the opening direction
sector: injection-molds
applies_when: When designing an injection-molded plastic part
not_when: A face that forms the parting plane or a feature with a slide
recognize: A face parallel to the mold opening with no angle
typical_error: A part that does not come out of the mold, gets scratched or deforms when ejected
related:
- design-tool-reaches-the-feature
sources:
- "Hubs, Injection molding design guide (read)."
- "Protolabs, injection molding design guidelines (read): values of a specific manufacturer."
when:
  stages: [detail]
  ops: [draft]
---
- Hubs: recommended minimum angle of 2°; on features more than 50 mm high, 1° more for every 25 mm. Textures call for approximately 1° to 2° additional.
- Protolabs: 0.5° on vertical faces; 2° in most cases; 3° for shut-off and light textures.
- When in doubt, start from 2° and confirm with the molder, especially if there is texture.
