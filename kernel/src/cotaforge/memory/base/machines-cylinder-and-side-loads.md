id: machines-cylinder-and-side-loads
type: practice
title: Cylinder connected to a guided load, with a floating joint or spherical bearing, and alignment checked over the whole stroke
sector: special-machines
applies_when: When connecting a cylinder (pneumatic or hydraulic) to a load that is already guided
not_when: The load is the cylinder's guide and its type and permissible side force are sized by the manufacturer
recognize: A rod screwed directly to a load that moves along a linear guide
typical_error: Rigid connection; the side load and the misalignment wear the rod seal and bearing
related:
- design-side-load-off-the-actuator
sources:
- "Fluid Power World, Hydraulic cylinder design: preventing side loading (read; popular-science article, not a standard or a catalog)."
---
- A spherical bearing at the rod end compensates part of the side load; trunnions follow the side load and reduce its effect; a stop tube or additional support gives the rod bearing and reduces its deflection.
- "Correct machine alignment is critical when installing a cylinder": if the cylinder does not push the load square, the rod flexes and generates side load.
- There are no figures in this entry: the permissible side load is in the cylinder manufacturer's catalog (not consulted).
