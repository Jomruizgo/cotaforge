id: machining-inner-radius-and-cavity-depth
type: practice
title: Inside vertical radius greater than or equal to one third of the depth; cavity no deeper than 4 times its width
sector: precision-machining
applies_when: When designing a milled pocket or cavity
not_when: A pocket made by electrical discharge machining
recognize: A sharp inside corner in a deep pocket
typical_error: A sharp inside corner that the end mill cannot make without rounding or without a fragile tool
related:
- design-tool-reaches-the-feature
sources:
- "Hubs, How to design parts for CNC machining (read)."
---
- Recommended inside vertical radius: one third of the cavity depth or greater.
- Recommended cavity depth: up to 4 times the width. Cavities deeper than 6 tool diameters are considered deep and call for a special tool.
- Floor radius: 0.5 mm, 1 mm or no radius.
- These are guidelines from a specific manufacturer: confirm with the shop that will make the part.
