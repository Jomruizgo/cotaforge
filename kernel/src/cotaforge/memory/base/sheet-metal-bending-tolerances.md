type: practice
title: Realistic bending tolerances
sector: sheet-metal
applies_when: When dimensioning positions that cross a bend
not_when: A different capability is agreed with the shop
recognize: A position across a bend with a tolerance tighter than the bend can hold
typical_error: Demanding across a bend a tolerance that the bending cannot sustain
related:
- design-tolerance-the-process-holds
sources:
- "Protolabs, Design guidelines for sheet metal fabrication (read): values of a specific manufacturer."
- "Hubs, Sheet metal fabrication design guide (read): no figures."
---
- Protolabs: ±1° on all bend angles; form height (from the top face of the sheet to that of the form) ±0.012 in (0.304 mm).
- Protolabs, bend to hole on a single surface: 0.015 in (0.38 mm) in material less than 0.13 in thick and 0.025 in (0.635 mm) in thicker material.
- Hubs: the bending tolerance is somewhat larger than that of CNC machining; it gives no figures.
- These are values from a specific manufacturer: do not generalize them; use the shop's measured capability when it exists.
