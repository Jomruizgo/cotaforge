---
id: molds-bosses-and-radii
type: practice
title: Bosses with a diameter proportional to the screw and radii at the corners
sector: injection-molds
applies_when: When designing a cylindrical protrusion for a screw or insert and the corners of the part
not_when: An overmolded insert with other supplier rules
recognize: A boss with the same wall as the part, or a sharp inside corner
typical_error: Sink marks on the face opposite the boss; cracks at sharp corners
sources:
- "Hubs, Injection molding design guide (read)."
- "Boss wall thickness: not included; the guide read gives no figure and the one seen in a summary could not be checked."
---
- Outside diameter of the boss: 2 times the nominal diameter of the screw or insert; its inside diameter is the core diameter of the screw.
- The boss hole goes down to the level of the base wall, to keep the thickness uniform.
- Corner radii: inside at least 0.5 times the wall thickness; outside equal to the inside radius plus the wall thickness.
