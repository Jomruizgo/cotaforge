type: practice
title: No threads exposed to the product; joints and O-rings without gaps
sector: hygienic-equipment
applies_when: When choosing fasteners, seals and joints in product zones
not_when: An O-ring deformed flush on the product side
recognize: A thread, screw or O-ring in contact with the product
typical_error: A thread or a gasket groove where residue accumulates
related:
- design-outlet-for-what-accumulates
sources:
- "EHEDG Doc 8, Hygienic equipment design criteria, 2nd edition, 2004, section 6.2 (read)."
- "MIT OCW 2.75 (Culpepper), Design of constraints in precision systems, 2001: an exact kinematic coupling does not admit sealing contact (read)."
---
- Eliminate product contact with threads; design seals and joints without crevices where dirt accumulates.
- Avoid O-rings in contact with the product, unless they deform to form a flush seal on the product side.
- An exact kinematic coupling does not admit a seal, so it may be incompatible with the hygienic seal.
