title: Avoid horizontal surfaces; slope to one side
sector: hygienic-equipment
applies_when: When defining the exterior and interior geometry of a piece of equipment
not_when: A tank whose complete emptying is guaranteed by other means
recognize: A flat horizontal surface that can retain liquid
typical_error: A flat bottom or an upward-facing U profile that retains wash water
related:

sources:
- "EHEDG Doc 8, Hygienic equipment design criteria, 2nd edition, 2004, sections 6.4 and 6.5 (read)."
- "Slope figure: the source does not give it; bioprocess conventions were seen only in supplier listings and are not included."
---
- "The exterior and interior of all equipment and pipework must be self-draining or drainable and easy to clean. Horizontal surfaces must be avoided; surfaces must always slope to one side. On exterior surfaces, liquid must be directed away from the main product area."
- Condensation is avoided whenever possible and, if not, diverted away from the product.
- The document gives no numeric slope: agree on it with the customer or with the applicable standard.
