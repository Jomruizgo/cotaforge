---
id: sheet-metal-bend-relief
type: practice
title: Bend relief where there is material on both sides or where the bend ends at a flat face
sector: sheet-metal
applies_when: When ending a flange against another flange or against a flat face
not_when: A bend that runs to the free edge of the part with no material beside it
recognize: Two flanges that meet without a notch, or a bend that ends at a flat contour
typical_error: Material that tears or bulges at the junction
related:
- design-tool-reaches-the-feature
sources:
- "Hubs, Sheet metal fabrication design guide (read)."
- "Protolabs, Bend relief (read)."
---
- Hubs: a cut on each side of the bend with a width not less than the thickness.
- Protolabs: width of at least 0.030 in (0.762 mm) and depth of at least 0.015 in (0.381 mm) beyond the end of the bend radius; for a 90° bend, the relief is the thickness plus the bend radius.
- Prefer rounded ends to sharp corners: they reduce tearing and crack propagation.
- These are guidelines from specific manufacturers: confirm with the shop.
