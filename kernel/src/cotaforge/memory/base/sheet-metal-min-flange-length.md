---
id: sheet-metal-min-flange-length
type: practice
title: Minimum flange length
sector: sheet-metal
applies_when: When defining the height of a bent flange
not_when: A different die is agreed with the shop
recognize: A very short flange next to a radius
typical_error: A flange that does not rest on the die and forms badly or is rejected
related:
- design-tool-reaches-the-feature
sources:
- "Protolabs, Design guidelines for sheet metal fabrication (read)."
- "Hubs, Sheet metal fabrication design guide (read)."
---
- Protolabs: minimum flange length of at least 4 times the thickness.
- Hubs: flange height of at least twice the thickness plus the bend radius.
- The sources differ: when in doubt, take the larger value and confirm with the die of the shop that will make the part.
