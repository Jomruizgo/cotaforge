id: fixtures-locators-on-machined-surfaces
type: practice
title: Flat buttons on machined surfaces, domed buttons on unmachined surfaces
sector: fixtures
applies_when: When choosing and mounting the supports that touch the workpiece
not_when: A support with a ball joint or self-leveling chosen on purpose
recognize: A flat support against a rough face, or a machined face resting on a domed support
typical_error: Resting a rough face on a flat button; the part rocks and its location changes with each burr
related:
- design-one-support-per-degree-of-freedom
- design-outlet-for-what-accumulates
sources:
- "Applied Engineering, Jig and Fixture Design (summary of E. K. Hendriksen, Jig and Fixture Design Manual, 1973): buttons, pins and reliefs (read)."
---
- Flat buttons for machined surfaces; domed for unmachined surfaces.
- Press fit, preferable to threading for accuracy and vibration resistance; with a through or relief hole to install and remove. Preferred fit according to the source: class LN 3 to FN 2.
- Relief around pins and buttons for chips and dirt.
- Pins used as side stops: only with light loads, to avoid a large bending moment.
