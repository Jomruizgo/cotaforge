---
id: welded-welds-near-neutral-axis-balanced
type: practice
title: Welds near the neutral axis and balanced on both sides
sector: welded-structures
applies_when: When deciding the position and distribution of the welds on a section or frame
not_when: A single-pass joint on a symmetric section
recognize: Welds all on one side of a section, or far from the neutral axis
typical_error: Welding on one side only; the part curves toward the side of the welds
related:
- design-distortion-from-heat-or-forming
sources:
- "TWI, Distortion prevention by design (read)."
---
- The closer the weld is to the neutral axis, the smaller the leverage of the shrinkage forces and the final distortion.
- Balance the weld about the center of the joint and, whenever possible, weld alternating sides instead of finishing one side first.
