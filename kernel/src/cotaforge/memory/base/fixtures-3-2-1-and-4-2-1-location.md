type: practice
title: 3-2-1 location is the minimum; it does not guarantee stability
sector: fixtures
applies_when: When defining the supports of a part in a fixture
not_when: The part is located by a complete fit or by a shape that closes all movements
recognize: Three supports under a tall part or with the clamping force outside the support triangle
typical_error: Taking a 3-2-1 as stable with the clamp outside the triangle; the part tips over when machining

---
- 3-2-1: three supports on the base, two on a side face and one on an end face. It is the minimum location requirement: it gives equilibrium but not necessarily stability.
- 4-2-1: gives equilibrium and stability.
- Note (engineering judgment, no source of its own): the fourth base support reintroduces the risk of overconstraint of the linked principle; it is justified by stability and is made adjustable or ground to a single height.
