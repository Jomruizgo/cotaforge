---
id: transmission-bearing-fit-by-load
type: practice
title: Ring fit according to which ring rotates relative to the load
sector: power-transmission
applies_when: When choosing the tolerances of a bearing seat on the shaft and in the housing
not_when: Bearings with an adapter sleeve or with a fit specified by the manufacturer
recognize: A bearing seat with no declared tolerance, or the same on both seats
typical_error: A ring with rotating load mounted with clearance; it rolls on the seat and wears it
sources:
- "NTN, bearing catalog, fits (the text of the rule was read; the tolerance zone tables were badly laid out and are not used)."
---
- The ring with rotating load gets an interference fit, to prevent slipping; the ring with stationary load may get a loose fit, to ease assembly and axial displacement.
- The specific tolerance zones of the shaft and housing (by size and load) are not given here: they are taken from the bearing manufacturer's table, and the fit is declared in full on the drawing.
