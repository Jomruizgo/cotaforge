title: Austenitic stainless steel as the base; beware of chlorides, temperature and porous polymers
sector: hygienic-equipment
applies_when: When choosing the material of a part in contact with product or with detergents
not_when: The customer sets the material
recognize: A material with no designation on a contact part
typical_error: A material that pits with chlorides, or a porous polymer that is hard to clean
sources:

- AISI 304 / 304L (DIN 1.4307) for solutions of pH between 6.5 and 8, low chlorides (up to about 50 mg/l) and low temperature (up to about 25 °C). If chloride and temperature exceed approximately twice those values, more resistance to crevice and pitting corrosion is needed: AISI 316 (valves, pump housings, rotors, shafts) or 316L (DIN 1.4435; pipes and vessels, for its better weldability). Near 150 °C, even 316 may suffer stress corrosion with chlorides.
- PTFE can be porous and hard to clean; certain modified PTFEs and fully fluorinated fluoropolymers, such as PFA, do comply.
- Elastomers for seals, gaskets and rings in the food industry: EPDM (does not resist oils or fats), FKM, HNBR, NR, NBR, VMQ and FFKM.
