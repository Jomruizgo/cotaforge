---
id: fixtures-fixture-tolerance
type: practice
title: The fixture tolerance is a fraction of the part tolerance
sector: fixtures
applies_when: When defining the tolerance of the locators, bushings and seats of a fixture relative to the tolerance of the part it holds
not_when: The customer or the internal standard sets a different ratio
recognize: A fixture with the same tolerance as the part, or much more relaxed
typical_error: Giving the fixture the same tolerance as the part; the fixture consumes all the tolerance and good parts are rejected
related:
- design-tolerance-the-process-holds
sources:
- "Applied Engineering, Jig and Fixture Design (summary of E. K. Hendriksen, Jig and Fixture Design Manual, 1973): 40 % rule of thumb (read)."
---
- Rule of thumb: the fixture tolerance is approximately 40 % of the tolerance of the finished part. Example from the source: part with ±0.20 mm, fixture ±0.20 × 0.40 = ±0.08 mm.
- It is a single source, a summary of a 1973 manual: confirm the ratio with the customer or with their internal standard.
