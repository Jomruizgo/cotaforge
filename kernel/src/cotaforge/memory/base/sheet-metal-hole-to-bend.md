---
id: sheet-metal-hole-to-bend
type: practice
title: Distance from hole to bend
sector: sheet-metal
applies_when: When placing a hole or slot near a bend
not_when: The hole is made after bending
recognize: A hole less than 2 thicknesses from the start of the bend radius
typical_error: The hole becomes oval or acts as an unwanted relief when bending
related:
- design-tool-reaches-the-feature
- design-support-and-edge-distance
sources:
- "Hubs, Sheet metal fabrication design guide (read)."
- "Protolabs, Design guidelines for sheet metal fabrication (read)."
---
- Hubs: holes at a distance of at least 2 times the thickness from the start of the bend radius.
- Protolabs: features closer than about 4 times the thickness to a bend risk distortion.
- The two sources differ: when in doubt, take the larger distance and confirm with the shop that will make the part. Other manufacturer guides (Fictiv, Xometry) were seen only in summary and are not used here.
