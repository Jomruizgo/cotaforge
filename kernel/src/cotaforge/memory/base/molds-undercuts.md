---
id: molds-undercuts
type: practice
title: Undercuts with a lead-in angle, or a slide
sector: injection-molds
applies_when: When designing a feature that prevents straight opening of the mold
not_when: An undercut that is avoided by redesigning the part
recognize: A hook, a shoulder or a side hole that blocks ejection
typical_error: An undercut with no way out that forces an expensive slide or breaks the part on ejection
related:
- design-tool-reaches-the-feature
sources:
- "Hubs, Injection molding design guide (read)."
---
- Undercut that can be released by force: lead-in angle of 30° to 45°; flexible plastics (PP, HDPE, nylon) tolerate undercuts of up to 5 % of their diameter.
- If that is not possible, a slide is used in the mold or the feature is redesigned. The size limits of a slide depend on the mold supplier: consult them with the supplier.
