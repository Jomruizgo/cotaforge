type: practice
title: 'Hollow section: no open end; if closed, with a vent'
sector: welded-structures
applies_when: When finishing the end of a tube or hollow section
not_when: An end closed with a plug or a weld against another part with an air outlet
recognize: A tube that ends butt against another or in the air with the hollow section visible
typical_error: Leaving the end open, where dirt enters and it rusts, or closing the hollow completely with no vent
examples:
- sector: welded-structures
  text: 'The L-shaped corners of a tube frame are mitered and welded: no open end.'
  validated: false
sources:
- 'EHEDG Doc 8 (2004), section 6.7: supports and sections do not retain water or dirt (read).'
- 'Vents of a sealed hollow volume (hot air when welding, immersion when galvanizing): common knowledge, not verified against a galvanizers guide.'
when:
  roles:
  - estructura
  - soldado
  stages:
  - detail
---
- An open end in view lets dirt and moisture in: it is closed with a plug, a cap or joined with a miter.
- Warning: a fully sealed hollow volume needs venting; with the air trapped, welding and galvanizing can burst it or leave it with moisture inside. When closing the hollow, a vent is provided, and a drain if liquid can enter.
