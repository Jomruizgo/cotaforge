title: Useful thread length in a threaded hole
sector: precision-machining
applies_when: When defining a threaded hole
not_when: A catalog threaded insert with its own table is used
recognize: A threaded hole deeper than 3 times the diameter
typical_error: Threading deeper than is useful
related:
- design-tool-reaches-the-feature
sources:
- "Hubs, How to design parts for CNC machining (read)."
- "Protolabs, Design for machining toolkit (read)."
- "Thread engagement by material (steel, aluminum): not included; the figures seen were from secondary sources."
---
- Hubs: recommended thread length of 3 times the nominal diameter; most of the load is carried by the first teeth, up to about 1.5 diameters, so a longer thread adds little.
- Protolabs: threads deeper than 3 diameters may leave material unremoved.
- The engagement needed in soft materials (aluminum, plastic) against a stronger screw is not covered here: use the screw manufacturer's table or an insert.
