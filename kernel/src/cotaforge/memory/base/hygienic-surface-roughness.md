id: hygienic-surface-roughness
type: practice
title: Ra of 0.8 µm or better on extensive product surfaces; topography governs
sector: hygienic-equipment
applies_when: When specifying the finish of product-contact surfaces
not_when: Dry, low-risk products according to EHEDG's own criterion
recognize: A contact surface with no specified Ra, or a finish that masks cracks
typical_error: Polishing to lower the Ra and leaving pits or cracks, or specifying an Ra without checking the topography
sources:
- "EHEDG Doc 8, Hygienic equipment design criteria, 2nd edition, 2004, section 6.3 and table 2 (read)."
- "The current edition is EHEDG guideline GL 8, 4th edition (December 2025); its text was not read: confirm that the values did not change."
---
- Extensive contact surfaces must have Ra 0.8 µm or better, although cleanability depends on the finishing technique. A higher Ra may be accepted if tests demonstrate cleanability.
- Cold-rolled steel has an Ra of 0.2 to 0.5 µm and normally does not need polishing if it has no pits, folds or cracks.
- Approximate Ra by treatment (table 2): hot-rolled > 4 µm; cold-rolled 0.2 to 0.5; pickled 0.5 to 1.0; micro-blasted < 1; glass-bead or ceramic blasting < 1.2. Abrasive polishing by grit: 500, 0.1 to 0.25; 240, 0.2 to 0.5; 120, up to 1.1; 60, up to 3.5.
- Electropolishing rounds peaks without necessarily improving Ra: it is the topography that governs cleanability.
