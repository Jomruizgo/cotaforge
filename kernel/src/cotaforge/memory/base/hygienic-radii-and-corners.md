- "Corners should preferably have a radius equal to or greater than 6 mm; the minimum radius is 3 mm. Sharp corners (≤ 90°) must be avoided."
- If the corner is a sealing point, it must be as sharp as possible; a broken edge or a radius of 0.2 mm avoids damaging elastomeric seals with thermal cycling.
- Values from the 2004 edition: check against the current edition.
