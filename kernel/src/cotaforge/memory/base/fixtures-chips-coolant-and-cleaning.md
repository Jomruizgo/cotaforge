id: fixtures-chips-coolant-and-cleaning
type: practice
title: Leave an outlet for chips and coolant, and visibility of the supports
sector: fixtures
applies_when: When designing a fixture used on a machine with chips or coolant
not_when: A clean inspection or assembly fixture
recognize: A support at the bottom of a closed pocket, or a bearing face with no side outlet
typical_error: Chips under the part that displace its support; the part ends up badly located without it being noticed
related:
- design-outlet-for-what-accumulates
sources:
- "Applied Engineering, Jig and Fixture Design (summary of E. K. Hendriksen, Jig and Fixture Design Manual, 1973): tooling design considerations (read)."
---
- Wear resistance, ease of replacement, visibility, accessibility for cleaning and use, and protection against chips and dirt are tooling design considerations.
- Leave relief around pins and buttons so that chips and dirt do not displace the support.
