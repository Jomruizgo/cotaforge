---
id: welded-minimum-weld-size-and-passes
type: practice
title: Weld of the minimum size that meets the requirement, minimum preparation and intermittent welding where strength allows
sector: welded-structures
applies_when: When specifying the size and type of weld of a joint
not_when: A fatigue or leaktight joint or one with a code that requires a continuous weld
recognize: An oversized or continuous weld where a smaller or intermittent one would do
typical_error: Overwelding "for safety"; more heat, more distortion and more cost without gaining strength
related:
- design-distortion-from-heat-or-forming
sources:
- "TWI, Distortion prevention by design (read)."
---
- The weld section must be kept as small as possible.
- In an unrestrained joint, angular distortion is approximately proportional to the number of passes.
- Reduce to a minimum the preparation angle and the root opening, provided the weld can be made properly.
- Use intermittent welding instead of continuous when strength allows; poor joint fit-up and overwelding increase distortion.
