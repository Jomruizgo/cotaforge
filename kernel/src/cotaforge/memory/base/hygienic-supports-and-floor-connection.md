---
id: hygienic-supports-and-floor-connection
type: practice
title: Supports that do not retain water or dirt; equipment sealed to the floor with no gaps or with cleanable clearance
sector: hygienic-equipment
applies_when: When designing legs, supports and the connection of the equipment to the floor or the wall
not_when: Suspended equipment with no contact with the floor
recognize: An open hollow leg, or an inaccessible gap between the equipment and the floor
typical_error: A leg with an open profile where water accumulates; a gap under the equipment that is impossible to clean
related:
- design-outlet-for-what-accumulates
- design-access-for-assembly-and-maintenance
sources:
- "EHEDG Doc 8, Hygienic equipment design criteria, 2nd edition, 2004, sections 6.5 and 6.7 (read)."
---
- Supports are made and installed so that no water or dirt remains on the surface or inside them; consider galvanic reactions between dissimilar metals.
- The equipment and support structures are sealed to the bearing surface (floor, walls) with no pockets or gaps; the clearance between the equipment and the civil works must be adequate for cleaning and inspection.
