---
id: machining-min-wall-thickness
type: practice
title: Minimum thickness of a machined wall
sector: precision-machining
applies_when: When designing machined thin walls, ribs and protrusions
not_when: Micro-fabrication parts with their own process
recognize: A thin wall of great height
typical_error: A wall that vibrates or breaks when machined
related:
- design-tool-reaches-the-feature
sources:
- "Hubs, How to design parts for CNC machining (read)."
- "Protolabs, Design for machining toolkit (read)."
---
- Hubs: recommended minimum thickness of 0.8 mm in metals and 1.5 mm in plastics.
- Protolabs: walls of 0.020 in (0.51 mm) or less are considered thin-wall geometry and risk breaking when machined.
