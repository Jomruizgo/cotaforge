title: Clearance for fingers and tool, and loading mistake-proofing
sector: fixtures
applies_when: When shaping the fixture around the part and the operator's hands
not_when: Loading and unloading are automatic and there is no operator
recognize: A finger gap smaller than the hand, or a nearly symmetric part with no mistake-proofing
typical_error: A fixture in which the part is loaded turned or that cannot be unloaded because of burrs or warpage

- Recommended minimum finger clearance: 5/8 in (≈ 16 mm), more if a grip is needed. Protruding edges, corners and points rounded, blunted or covered. Consider the weight if the fixture is lifted or turned by hand.
- Mistake-proofing: notches, contours, pins and blocks that let the correctly placed part through and prevent the wrongly placed one. Burrs, warpage, heavy parts or parts added inside the fixture make unloading difficult: ejectors and moving parts help.
- Fixture on a T-slot table: use slots and not holes so that the nuts need not be removed; the locating key is aligned against one side of the slot only.
