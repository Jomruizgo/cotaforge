---
id: machining-drilled-hole-depth
type: practice
title: Depth of drilled holes
sector: precision-machining
applies_when: When defining the depth of a drilled hole
not_when: A deep hole made with a gun drill or an agreed special process
recognize: A blind hole deeper than 4 times its diameter
typical_error: A deep hole whose bottom cannot be cleaned or measured, or that drifts
related:
- design-tool-reaches-the-feature
sources:
- "Hubs, How to design parts for CNC machining (read)."
- "Protolabs, Design for machining toolkit (read)."
---
- Hubs: recommended depth of 4 times the nominal diameter; typical up to 10 times.
- Protolabs: holes deeper than 6 diameters may leave material unremoved.
- The sources differ: when in doubt, start from 4 diameters and consult the shop before going beyond 6.
