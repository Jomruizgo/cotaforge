title: Permanent metal-to-metal joints continuously welded and smooth; no metal-to-metal joints that retain dirt
sector: hygienic-equipment
applies_when: When defining how parts in contact with product are joined
not_when: A removable joint with a designed hygienic seal
recognize: Two metal parts joined by contact or by a discontinuous bead in a product zone
typical_error: An intermittent bead or a bolted metal-to-metal joint that leaves a crevice
related:
- design-outlet-for-what-accumulates
sources:
- "EHEDG Doc 8, Hygienic equipment design criteria, 2nd edition, 2004, sections 6.2 and 6.6 (read)."

- "Permanent metal-to-metal joints in contact with product must be continuously welded and free of imperfections."
- Welds on the non-product side are continuous and smooth enough to be cleaned.
- For pipework, automatic orbital welding is preferred; inert gas shielding on both sides reduces later polishing.
- Avoid metal-to-metal joints other than welds and steps due to misalignment in equipment and connections.
