---
id: molds-wall-thickness-and-transitions
type: practice
title: Uniform wall thickness within the resin's range and gradual transitions
sector: injection-molds
applies_when: When defining the thickness of an injection-molded part
not_when: A thick-walled part made with another process (for example, casting)
recognize: Walls of different thicknesses in the same part with no transition
typical_error: Sink marks, voids and warpage in the thick areas
related:
- design-distortion-from-heat-or-forming
sources:
- "Hubs, Injection molding design guide (read)."
- "Protolabs, injection molding design guidelines (read): ranges of a specific manufacturer."
---
- Constant wall whenever possible; avoid thick sections; smooth the change in thickness with a chamfer or radius of length equal to 3 times the thickness difference (Hubs).
- Recommended ranges by resin (Protolabs): ABS from 0.045 to 0.140 in (1.14 to 3.56 mm); polycarbonate from 0.040 to 0.150 in (1.02 to 3.81 mm); polypropylene from 0.035 to 0.150 in (0.89 to 3.81 mm). Confirm with the resin data sheet.
